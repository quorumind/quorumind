_Fictional example written for the Quorumind quality bench: the companies, people and figures are invented._

**Pixelforge Studio**, web agency, 14 rue des Exemples, 69000 Lyon, France
**Client:** Lotus Yoga Network, 12 studios
**Date:** 2 October 2026. This quote is valid for 30 days.

## 1. Purpose

Design and build a booking website for the 12 studios of the Lotus Yoga Network: members browse the class schedule, book and cancel classes, buy passes online and receive reminders.

## 2. Scope

- Responsive website for mobile and desktop, built on WordPress with a booking plugin adapted to your needs.
- Class schedule for each studio, managed by the studio managers.
- Online booking and cancellation of classes.
- Sale of 5-class and 10-class passes by card payment through Stripe.
- E-mail reminders 24 hours before each class.
- Back office for the network's staff.
- Data migration from the current tool if needed.
